## 🏢 SaaS & Commercial Platforms

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[NVIDIA Isaac Lab](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/isaac-sim/IsaacLab?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/stargazers)  

  **Unified and modular framework for robot learning**, BSD-3-Clause / Apache-2.0 licensed. **Built on NVIDIA Isaac Sim** for **photo-realistic scenes and fast, efficient simulation** . **Core objectives**: **Modularity** (easily customize and add new environments, robots, sensors), **Agility** (adapt to changing community needs), **Openness** (remain open-sourced), **Batteries-included** (ready-to-use environments, sensors, tasks) . **Key features**: **fast and accurate physics simulation via PhysX**, **tiled rendering APIs** for vectorized rendering, **domain randomization** for robustness, **cloud deployment support** . **Included robots**: Cartpole, Humanoid, Ant, **UR10, Franka, Allegro, Shadow Hand**, **Anybotics Anymal, Unitree A1/Go1/Go2, Boston Dynamics Spot**, **Unitree H1/G1**, **Crazyflie** . **The most comprehensive open-source robot learning framework** — GPU-accelerated with cloud support . 🚀



- **[PyRoboSimulator](https://github.com/pyrobosimulator/pyrobosimulator)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/pyrobosimulator/pyrobosimulator?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/pyrobosimulator/pyrobosimulator/stargazers)  

  **Modular robotics simulation framework with behavior trees, multi-agent coordination, and narrative engine**, open-source. **Behavior Trees**: Sequence, Selector, Parallel with **<1ms evaluation for 100+ agents** . **Navigation**: A* pathfinding with **heuristic caching, nav mesh, RVO collision avoidance**, **<1ms pathfinding** . **Agent Memory**: **Episodic, semantic, procedural, emotional** with decay and relationships . **Multi-Agent Communication**: Direct, broadcast, multicast with **priority queuing** and **range-based broadcasting** . **Narrative Simulation Engine**: **NLP-driven scenarios via Claude API**, 11 scenario types, **dynamic story branching** . **Real-to-Sim Bridge**: **ROS Bag parsing**, trajectory extraction, **sensor replay, sim-real validation** . **Curriculum Learning**: **Adaptive difficulty with 7-factor weighted model**, learner profiles, progressive scenarios . **Multi-Agent Coordination**: **6 formation types** (swarm, line, circle, grid, hierarchy, scout), **collective intelligence metrics** . **Fleet Learning**: Experience logging, **pattern identification, knowledge transfer, team performance analytics** . **The most feature-complete open-source multi-agent simulation framework** . 🎯



- **[OrcaGym](https://github.com/openverse-orca/OrcaGym)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openverse-orca/OrcaGym?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openverse-orca/OrcaGym/stargazers)  

  **Cloud-native distributed robotics simulation with Gym/Gymnasium compatibility**, open-source. **Multi-physics backends**: **MuJoCo (CPU), MuJoCoWarp (CUDA), Euler (multi-vendor GPU)** . **Distributed deployment** across heterogeneous compute nodes . **Ray tracing and 3DGS rendering** for vision-based RL tasks . **Multi-agent support** with native heterogeneous agent management . **Developed with OrcaStudio and OrcaLab platforms** . **Part of a complete data pipeline**: **OrcaManipulation** provides **data collection → synthesis → training → inference** . **Install via `pip install orca-gym`** with optional extras for RL, imitation learning, sensors, and devices . **The most distributed-ready open-source robotics simulation platform** . 🌐



- **[MicroDuck Lab Cloud](https://github.com/heranhe/microduck-lab-cloud)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/heranhe/microduck-lab-cloud?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/heranhe/microduck-lab-cloud/stargazers)  

  **Cloud-ready reinforcement learning and behavior suite for biped robots**, open-source. **Train MicroDuck on a Mac, watch it learn to walk, tidy toys, follow people, and play football in the browser** . **Cloud GPU support via Google Colab / Hugging Face** with **Chinese and English interfaces** . **Scenarios**: **Living room** (obstacles, ball, sensors), **Toy room** (find, carry, tidy), **Football field** (1v1/2v2/3v3), **Follow queue** (five ducks follow G1) . **Training analysis** with reward and episode length curves . **Action editing** with **keyframes, foot/hand IK, center of mass** . **Recording**: screenshots, videos, GIFs, and world recordings . **The most accessible cloud-ready RL robotics lab** . 🦆



- **[CommandAGI](https://pypi.org/project/commandagi/)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/commandagi/commandagi?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/commandagi/commandagi/stargazers)  

  **Launch real cloud computers and 3D robot simulations from Python**, open-source. **`pip install commandagi`** and **launch a GCE VM running a 3D physics world** in seconds . **Built-in scenes**: warehouse, house-on-fire, school . **Morphology-agnostic robot control** with generic vocabulary: `ctrl`, `actuator`, `ik`, `trajectory`, `describe` . **Multi-robot sessions** with **visibility control and capability granting** (viewer, operator) . **Stream camera frames as JPEG or numpy arrays** . **No agent required — you drive** . **The simplest open-source cloud robotics simulation API** . 🎮



- **[Gazebo](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/gazebosim/gz-sim?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/stargazers)  

  **Open-source 3D robotics simulator**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **The foundational open-source robotics simulator** . **Physics simulation** (ODE, Bullet, DART), **sensor simulation** (cameras, LiDAR, IMU), and **ROS/ROS 2 integration** . **Used by AWS RoboMaker WorldForge** for simulation world generation . **The most widely adopted open-source robotics simulator** . 🤖



- **[ROS 2](https://github.com/ros2/ros2)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ros2/ros2?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ros2/ros2/stargazers)  

  **Robot Operating System 2**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **The de facto standard middleware for robotics development** . **DDS-based communication**, real-time support, and **multi-platform compatibility** . **The foundation for most open-source and commercial robotics platforms** . 🔗



- **[MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/google-deepmind/mujoco?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/stargazers)  

  **Multi-Joint dynamics with Contact**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Fast and accurate physics simulation** for robotics and RL . **Used by MicroDuck Lab Cloud and OrcaGym** . **The preferred physics engine for reinforcement learning** . ⚡



- **[LeRobot](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/huggingface/lerobot?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/stargazers)  

  **Hugging Face's robotics library**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **End-to-end robot learning** with datasets, models, and training scripts . **Bridges the gap between ML and robotics** . **The most accessible open-source robot learning library** . 🤗



- **[Genesis](https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/Genesis)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Genesis-Embodied-AI/Genesis?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/Genesis/stargazers)  

  **Generative physics engine for robotics**, open-source. **Ultra-fast simulation** with **generative physics** . **Designed for embodied AI and robotics research** . **The most innovative open-source physics simulation engine** . 🌌
